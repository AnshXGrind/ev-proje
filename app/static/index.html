Data and method notes

What the available data can and cannot support.

01

Observed source fields

Charger coordinates, operator, reported ownership, connector type and rated power from the dated public inventory.

AVAILABLE · SNAPSHOT ONLY
02

Calculated spatial analysis

Approximate coordinate groups, DBSCAN clusters and straight-line coverage-gap candidates are computed from observed coordinates.

COMPUTED LOCALLY
03

Demand forecast

No charging-session time series or traffic observations are supplied; this version does not calculate a validated demand forecast.

DATA GAP
04

Grid hosting capacity

No local feeder topology, transformer ratings or peak loading supplied. Candidate sites require utility validation.

NOT VERIFIED
This is an engineering MVP for screening and decision support. It does not claim real-time charger status, measured EV demand, approved installation sites or verified grid feasibility.
